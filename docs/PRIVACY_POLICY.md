# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 2026**

## Introduction

MailVault ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our email archiving service.

## Information We Collect

### Microsoft Account Information
When you sign in with Microsoft, we receive:
- Your name and email address
- Access to your mailbox (with your permission)
- Access to your OneDrive (with your permission)

### Email Data
- We access your emails **only** to display them in the app and export them
- We **do not store** your emails on our servers
- Exported emails are saved to **your device** or **your OneDrive**

### Usage Data
We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve our service:
- Features used
- Error reports
- Performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Authenticate you with Microsoft services
- Display your email statistics and content
- Export and archive your emails to your chosen destination
- Improve our service

## Data Storage

### We Do NOT Store:
- ❌ Your emails or their contents
- ❌ Your email attachments
- ❌ Your Microsoft password

### We May Store:
- ✅ Account preferences (stored locally in your browser)
- ✅ Anonymous usage analytics

## Third-Party Services

We integrate with:
- **Microsoft Graph API** - To access your Microsoft 365 data
- **Microsoft OneDrive** - To save archived emails (optional)

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication
- No sensitive data is stored on our servers

## Your Rights

You can:
- **Revoke access** at any time via [Microsoft Account Settings](https://account.live.com/consent/Manage)
- **Delete** your local preferences by clearing browser data
- **Request information** about what data we access

## Children's Privacy

MailVault is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.
